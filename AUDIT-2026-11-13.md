## Bottom line

Security exposure is small: a static site with no backend, no secrets in the tree, no third-party scripts in the main app, and a clean dependency audit. The two security items worth real attention are both about the Nsyte copy (it presents itself as the real company's site, and its contact form has no spam protection once a relay is connected).

The work that matters most is in accessibility and layout. Three problems were reproduced and should be fixed first:

1. **A1**: tabbing to a project panel breaks the horizontal strip's position.
2. **U1**: any window 992px or wider that is taller than it is wide (iPad Pro portrait, a portrait monitor) gets panels wider than the screen.
3. **A2**: project boards cannot be zoomed on a phone, and most of them are images of small text.

## Security

### S1. The Nsyte copy presents itself as the real nsyte.com (Medium, Verified)

**Where:** `public/nsyte/index.html` `<head>` and page body.

**Evidence:** The page title is "Nsyte | NIOP Machine Vision System". The head still carries Nsyte's `google-site-verification` token, `data-wf-domain="www.nsyte.com"`, `data-wf-site` and `data-wf-page` IDs, and Nsyte's Open Graph title and description. The only on-page sign that this is a portfolio piece is the footer contact block and the popout. A visitor who lands at the top sees a real company's site with working "demo" buttons and a contact form, hosted on a different domain.

**Why it matters:** A cloned brand page with a data-collecting form on an unrelated domain is the pattern that phishing detectors and brand-protection services look for. It can also confuse a real Nsyte customer who arrives by a shared link. `noindex` keeps it out of search but does nothing for either of those.

**Fix:**
1. Add a persistent, visible notice at the top of the page (a slim fixed bar is enough): "Portfolio copy of nsyte.com, designed and built by Enright Design Foundry. Not the live Nsyte site." with a link back to the portfolio and one to nsyte.com.
2. Change `<title>` to something like "Nsyte website (portfolio copy) | Enright Design Foundry" and rewrite the OG and Twitter tags to match.
3. Remove the `google-site-verification` meta tag and the `data-wf-domain`, `data-wf-site`, `data-wf-page` attributes (confirm `webflow.js` interactions still run without them; if they do not, keep `data-wf-page` and `data-wf-site` only).
4. Keep `noindex` and the canonical link to nsyte.com. Do not add a `robots.txt` Disallow for `/nsyte/`; that would stop crawlers from reading the `noindex`.

**Done when:** The notice is visible at every scroll position on desktop and phone, and a view-source search for `google-site-verification` and `data-wf-domain` returns nothing.

**Decision for Jim:** confirm Nsyte is fine with a full copy of their site (including client logos: BMW, Bosch, Airbus, Pepsi, and others) being hosted publicly. A screen recording or a set of screenshots would carry no such question.

### S2. Contact form has no spam protection (Medium, Code)

**Where:** `public/nsyte/assets/mirror.js` lines 9 to 82.

**Evidence:** With `FORM_ENDPOINT` empty the form opens a `mailto:` link, which is harmless. The moment an endpoint is pasted in, `sendByRelay` posts whatever is submitted. There is no honeypot field, no time check, no captcha, and the endpoint is in public source.

**Fix (do this in the same change that sets the endpoint):**
1. Add a visually hidden honeypot input (`tabindex="-1"`, `autocomplete="off"`, `aria-hidden="true"`) and drop submissions where it has a value.
2. Reject submissions made less than about 2 seconds after the form was shown.
3. Turn on the relay's own spam filtering or captcha, and restrict the access key to `enrightdesignfoundry.com` if the relay supports domain locking.
4. If the Rock 5B is used as the relay instead, put it behind the existing Cloudflare Tunnel with rate limiting, and validate field lengths server-side.

**Done when:** A scripted POST with the honeypot filled is discarded and a normal submission arrives.

### S3. No Content Security Policy or referrer policy (Low, Code)

**Where:** `index.html`, `public/nsyte/index.html`.

**Evidence:** GitHub Pages cannot send custom response headers, so the only available control is a `<meta http-equiv>` tag, and there is none.

**Fix:** Inject at build time only (a small Vite `transformIndexHtml` plugin gated on `command === 'build'`, since the dev server needs inline scripts and websockets):

```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Security-Policy" content="default-src 'self'; script-src 'self'; style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'; img-src 'self' data: https://i.ytimg.com; font-src 'self'; connect-src 'self'; frame-src https://www.youtube-nocookie.com; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'self'; form-action 'self'">
<meta name="referrer" content="strict-origin-when-cross-origin">
```

Notes: `'unsafe-inline'` for styles is needed because Motion and the viewer write inline `style` attributes. The Nsyte page has two inline scripts and will need its own policy (move the scripts to files, or add their hashes, and add the relay host to `connect-src` and `form-action` once S2 is done). `frame-ancestors` is ignored in a meta tag, so clickjacking protection is not available on GitHub Pages; impact here is negligible because nothing on the site is a sensitive action.

**Done when:** The production build loads with zero CSP violations in the console on the home page, in an image viewer, in a playing video, and on `/nsyte/`.

### S4. Deploy workflow hardening (Low, Code)

**Where:** `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`.

**Evidence:** `pages: write` and `id-token: write` are granted at workflow level, so the build job (which runs `npm ci` and every dependency's install scripts) holds them too. Actions are referenced by moving tags (`@v4`, `@v3`).

**Fix:**
1. Set top-level `permissions: contents: read`. Give `pages: write` and `id-token: write` only to the `deploy` job.
2. Pin each action to a full commit SHA with the version in a trailing comment, and confirm each is on its current major version while doing so.
3. Add `.github/dependabot.yml` for `github-actions` and `npm` (weekly).
4. At cutover, change `BASE_PATH` to `/` as the comment already says.

**Done when:** The workflow run shows the build job token with read-only scope and the deploy still succeeds.

### S6. Public repository hygiene before the first push (Low, Code)

**Evidence:** The folder is not a git repository yet. `.gitignore` covers `node_modules`, `dist`, and `media-src/`. No `.env`, key, or archive files are present. Points to settle before `git init`:

1. **Fonts.** `src/assets/fonts/DDCHardware-Regular.woff2` and three Futura TTFs in `public/nsyte/assets/fonts/` are commercial typefaces. Committing them to a public repo redistributes the files, which is a separate question from web embedding. ### S7. Dev-server middleware can throw and does not confine paths (Low, Code)

**Where:** `vite.config.ts` lines 15 to 22.

**Evidence:** `decodeURIComponent(pathname)` throws on a malformed escape such as `/%E0%A4%A/`, and `path.join(publicDir, decoded, 'index.html')` accepts `..` segments. It only runs under `npm run dev` and only tests for a file's existence, so exposure is minimal, but `vite --host` would put it on the LAN.

**Fix:** Wrap the decode in `try/catch` and call `next()` on failure. Resolve the joined path and skip unless it starts with `publicDir + path.sep`.

### S8. Third-party requests (Info, Code)

`src/lib/preload.ts` line 56 fetches a poster from `i.ytimg.com` when a video thumbnail is hovered, touched, or focused, before any click. The player itself loads from `youtube-nocookie.com` only on click. This is a reasonable trade for a fast viewer. If a privacy statement is ever added, mention it. External links use `rel="noopener"`; adding `noreferrer` is optional.

### S9. Nsyte bundle still contains Webflow's form handler (Info, Verified)

**Where:** `public/nsyte/assets/js/webflow.b815c31d9.js` (557 KB) and `jquery-3.5.1.min.js`.

**Evidence:** On load the page made no requests outside its own origin. `webflow.js` still contains the Webflow form endpoint, and the only thing stopping a submission from going there is `mirror.js` intercepting `submit` in the capture phase. If `mirror.js` fails to load, the Webflow handler runs.

**Fix:** Make the form safe without relying on script order: remove `data-wf-page-id` and `data-wf-element-id` from the form in the HTML (the popout clone already strips them), set `action="#"`, and either strip the forms module from the bundle or load `mirror.js` before `webflow.js`.

**Done when:** With `mirror.js` blocked in devtools, submitting the form makes no network request to any other host.

**What is already right:** no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, no `eval`, no storage or cookie use, hash routes resolve through a strict lookup in `findProject` (no injection path), the one `innerHTML` in `mirror.js` is a constant string, YouTube uses the no-cookie host, and the lockfile is committed with `npm ci` in CI.

## Accessibility

References are to WCAG 2.2 success criteria.

### A1. Keyboard focus desyncs the horizontal strip (High, Verified)

**Where:** `src/components/DesktopGallery.tsx` line 110 (`sticky top-0 h-screen w-screen overflow-hidden`) and `ProjectPanel.tsx`.

**Evidence:** At 1024x768 with the page at `scrollY = 0`, focusing project panels 1, 4, 8, and 12 set the sticky container's `scrollLeft` to 1049, 3353, 6425, and 9369 while `window.scrollY` stayed 0 and the strip's transform stayed at none. An `overflow: hidden` box is still scrollable by the browser, and focus scrolls it. From then on the strip is offset by that amount on top of the scroll-driven transform: wheel scrolling starts from the wrong place and the end of the strip runs into blank space. Any keyboard user hits this on the sixth Tab press. (The viewer already fixed the same class of bug with `overflow: clip`; see the comment in `index.css` line 77.)

**SC:** 2.1.1 Keyboard, 2.4.3 Focus Order, 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured.

**Fix:**
1. Change the sticky container to `overflow-clip` so the browser cannot scroll it.
2. That alone would leave focused panels off-screen, so add a focus handler: when a panel (or any focusable inside the strip) receives focus, compute its left offset within the frame and set `window.scrollTo({ top: offsetLeft * ratio })`, where `ratio = (dims.height - innerHeight) / dims.travel` (the same ratio the wheel handler uses). Center the panel when it is narrower than the viewport. Use `behavior: 'instant'` under reduced motion.
3. Apply the same handler to the nav column's controls so Shift+Tab back to DESIGN / VIDEO brings the nav into view.
4. While there, make Left / Right arrows step one panel at a time rather than 100px (plan section 5, item 5), and let Enter open (already works, since panels are buttons).

**Done when:** Tabbing from the hero through all 12 panels and back keeps `document.querySelector('main > div').scrollLeft === 0` throughout, each focused panel is fully inside the viewport, and wheel scrolling afterward continues from the focused panel. Repeat in Firefox and Safari.

### A2. Pinch zoom is blocked in the viewer; boards are unreadable on phones (High, Verified)

**Where:** `src/styles/index.css` lines 85 to 88 (`.viewer { touch-action: none }`), `ProjectViewer.tsx` touch handlers (lines 207 to 232, `e.preventDefault()` on `touchmove`).

**Evidence:** At 375x812 the Tesar Drive board renders at 375x324 CSS pixels. Every design project's main view is a board containing small text, and there is no way to enlarge it: pinch is suppressed, there is no zoom control, and tapping closes the viewer. The rebuild plan lists pinch zoom as a Priority 1 viewer feature (section 4.5).

**SC:** 1.4.4 Resize Text, 1.4.5 Images of Text, 1.4.10 Reflow.

**Fix:** Add zoom to `ImageStage` (and `DocumentStage` pages):
- Touch: pinch to zoom and one-finger pan while zoomed; double-tap toggles fit and 2x. Set `touch-action: pinch-zoom` only if using native zoom; a transform-based zoom inside the stage is more predictable alongside the existing pull-to-swap gesture. Disable pull-to-swap while zoomed in.
- Desktop: see U5 for the click behavior decision. At minimum add a zoom toggle button in the controls and `+` / `-` keys.
- Keep the viewport meta tag as it is (it does not disable zoom; good).

**Done when:** On a real phone, body text on the Army Futures Command board can be enlarged until readable and panned edge to edge, and closing still works with the X.

### A3. Images of text have no usable text alternative (Medium, Code)

**Where:** `src/content/projects.json`.

**Evidence:**

- The 16-page Army Futures Command presentation exposes only "page N of 16".
- `phantom-5` and `hands` have empty `description`; the DESCRIPTION button opens to a title and one line.
- Wrong alts: `project-convergence` thumb says "PC20: Brand Design"; `hackthemachine-virtual` thumb and the `hackthemachine` video thumb both say "HACKtheMACHINE NavalX: Event Design"; `tempest` thumb says "Brand Identity" where the discipline is "Brand Design".

For the presentation, either add a per-page `alt` array to `DocumentMedia` or offer the source PDF through the existing `link.download` mechanism so screen-reader users get real text. Fill the two empty descriptions and correct the alts listed above. Hide the DESCRIPTION button, or show only the title block, when `description` is empty (see U12).

**Done when:** No project has an empty description, no alt names a different project, and the presentation has either page alts or a PDF link.

### A4. Looping motion ignores reduced-motion and cannot be paused (Medium, Verified in part)

**Where and evidence:**
- `src/components/Lottie.tsx`: the Nsyte thumbnail loops whenever it is on screen, with no check of `prefers-reduced-motion` (Code).
- `design/full/pc_full.webp`: a 3.0 MB animated WebP that loops in the viewer with no pause (Code).
- `public/nsyte/index.html`: two background videos with `autoplay loop` and no controls, both playing (Verified). The page has only 2 reduced-motion rules, covering the benefit icons.
- The hero scroll hint loops forever; it does stop under reduced motion (Code).

**SC:** 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide; 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions.

**Fix:** In `LottieLoop`, when `useReducedMotion()` is true, call `goToAndStop` on a representative frame and never play. Convert `pc_full` to a muted looping `<video>` with a poster and a visible pause button (also fixes U3), paused by default under reduced motion. On the Nsyte page, pause both videos under reduced motion in `mirror.js` and add a small pause/play button. Stop the hero hint after 3 cycles.

**Done when:** With "Reduce motion" on in macOS settings, nothing on either page moves without user input.

### A5. Description text cannot be scrolled by keyboard (Medium, Verified in part)

**Where:** `ProjectViewer.tsx` `Description` (lines 741 to 782).

**Evidence:** The section is `overflow-y-auto` with a 45dvh cap and `tabIndex` of -1 (measured). It is also `pointer-events-none` with only the inner text column re-enabled, so a wheel over the gradient beside the text is swallowed by the viewer's wheel handler. At the sizes tested the text fit without scrolling; on a short landscape phone, or with the longer entries (Hack the Port, Army Futures Command) at larger text sizes, it will overflow.

**SC:** 2.1.1 Keyboard.

**Fix:** Give the section `tabIndex={0}` and a visible focus ring, and let Arrow / Page / Space scroll it when focused (the dialog's `onKeyDown` already exempts `#project-description`). Drop `pointer-events-none` on the scroller itself and keep click-through only on the transparent top padding.

### A6. Single-key shortcut and broad key capture (Low, Code)

**Where:** `ProjectViewer.tsx` `onKeyDown` (lines 276 to 298).

**Evidence:** `D` toggles the description from anywhere in the dialog, and Space, Arrow Up/Down, Page Up/Down, Home, and End are intercepted across the whole dialog to drive view swapping.

**SC:** 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts.

**Fix:** Keep the behavior but ignore `D` when the event target is a link, button, or text control, and do not intercept Home / End unless a document view is showing. Mention the shortcut in the button's accessible description (`aria-keyshortcuts="D"`).

### A7. DESIGN / VIDEO buttons have no fallback and a weak active state (Medium, Code)

**Where:** `src/components/LottieButton.tsx`, `useLottie.ts`.

**Evidence:** The button's only visible content is a Lottie SVG that appears after a dynamic import of lottie-web plus a 641 KB or 765 KB JSON fetch. Until then, or if either request fails, the button is an empty box on the sand column. The selected category is shown only by the Lottie resting on its "pressed" frame, a shift of a few pixels. Screen readers are fine (`aria-label`, `aria-pressed`).

**SC:** 1.4.1 Use of Color (by analogy: state conveyed by a subtle cue), 4.1.2 for robustness.

**Fix:** Render a static fallback inside the button (the first frame exported as an SVG or WebP, or the word in DDC Hardware) and fade the Lottie in over it once `DOMLoaded` fires. Add an unmistakable selected treatment independent of the animation, such as an ink bar beside the active button. This is also the moment to settle plan decision 4: rebuilding the press as SVG and CSS removes 1.4 MB and the fallback problem together.

**Done when:** With `/lottie/*.json` blocked in devtools, both buttons are visible, labeled, and operable, and the active one is obvious.

### A9. Focus indicators (Low, Check)

- The panel focus ring is a 3px sand inset outline over arbitrary artwork (`ProjectPanel.tsx` line 25). On light thumbnails such as the cream Nsyte tile it will be hard to see. Use a two-tone ring: sand outline plus an ink `box-shadow` inside it (SC 2.4.13, 1.4.11).
- The scroll hint button and the CALL / email links define no focus style and rely on the browser default against sand. Confirm it is visible in Firefox and Safari, or add `focus-visible:outline-2 outline-ink outline-offset-4`.

### A10. Viewer control contrast over light artwork (Low, Check)

**Where:** `ProjectViewer.tsx` `EXTRA_BUTTON` and the DESCRIPTION button: sand text on `bg-ink/40`, 12px on phones (measured).

Sand on solid ink is about 8:1, which is fine. Over a light board the 40% ink fill drops well below that, and sand text on a cream image can fall under 4.5:1. Raise the fill to `bg-ink/85` or solid ink, and consider 13px minimum for the labels.

### A11. Structure and semantics (Low, Verified in part)

- With the description open the dialog contains two `<h2>` elements with the same text (Verified). Make the visible one the heading and point the dialog at it with `aria-labelledby`; drop the hidden one, or keep it only as the focus target with `role="presentation"` semantics removed from the outline.
- `aria-controls="project-description"` points at an id that does not exist while closed (Verified). Add it only when open, or keep the section mounted and hidden.
- `document.title` never changes. Set it to "<Project> | Enright Design Foundry" while a project is open so history entries and screen-reader page titles are meaningful.
- There is no skip link. Add "Skip to work" as the first focusable element; on desktop it should run the same scroll as the hero hint.
- `MobileContactBar` sits inside `<main>` with no landmark. Wrap it in `<header>`.
- `Preloader` is `aria-hidden` but everything behind it is focusable while it covers the page. Set `inert` on the gallery until the preloader leaves (or remove the preloader, see U2).

### A12. Nsyte copy (Low, Verified)

- `<html>` has no `lang`. Add `lang="en"`.
- All five demo / trial buttons are `<a href="#">` with Lottie-only content and no accessible name. Add `role="button"` and an `aria-label` ("Request a demo", "Start a free trial") to each `[data-mirror-popout]` element; `mirror.js` can do this in `ready()`.
- Four `<h1>` elements and no landmarks. Since this is a faithful copy, demoting three of the h1s and wrapping the content in `<main>` is enough.
- Form inputs have no `autocomplete`. Add `name` and `email` (SC 1.3.5). The popout itself is well built: focus moves in, the page behind is inert, Esc closes, focus returns.

### A13. Viewport units and text size (Low, Code)

`MobileGallery.tsx` line 30 sizes the hero at `95vh`, which jumps when a phone's address bar hides. Use `95svh`. All viewer text is fixed in px; that is acceptable since browser zoom works on desktop, but it raises the stakes on A2 for phones.

**What is already right:** `lang="en"` and a real `h1`; panels are buttons with clear names; the inactive category is both `aria-hidden` and `inert`; the viewer is a native modal `<dialog>` that takes focus on open and returned it to the originating panel on close (verified); Esc closes the description first, then the viewer (verified); the back button closes the viewer; reduced-motion handling exists for the viewer, the category slide, and the hint; grayscale thumbnails reveal on keyboard focus as well as hover; body text contrast is about 8:1.

## UX and UI

### U1. Portrait windows 992px and wider get panels wider than the screen (High, Verified)

**Where:** `src/hooks/useMediaQuery.ts` line 15 (`(min-width: 992px)`), `DesktopGallery.tsx` line 135 (`w-[100vh]`).

**Evidence:** At 1024x1366 (iPad Pro 12.9 in portrait) the site uses the horizontal strip, and each panel measures 1366 x 1366 in a 1024px-wide viewport. No panel can be seen whole, the nav column is 273px, and the page is 15,641px tall. The same happens on a portrait desktop monitor or a tall, narrow-ish browser window.

**Fix:** Decide the layout on shape as well as width: `(min-width: 992px) and (min-aspect-ratio: 5/4)`. Everything else gets the vertical stack, which already works well at tablet sizes. Alternatively cap the panel at `min(100vh, 100vw - nav width)`, but the stack is the better experience on a touch tablet.

**Done when:** 1024x1366 and 1080x1920 render the vertical stack; 1024x768, 1440x900, and 1920x1080 render the strip.

### U2. Preloader blocks every visit and has no timeout (Medium, Code)

**Where:** `src/components/Preloader.tsx`.

**Evidence:** The sand screen stays until `window` fires `load`, then 500ms, then a 250ms slide. If any eager resource stalls, `load` never fires and the site stays covered indefinitely. The word LOADING only fades in after a full second, so on a fast connection the visitor sees a blank sand screen flash. It also plays on deep links and on every return visit.

**Fix (minimum):** Cap the wait (leave after 2.5s regardless), and skip it entirely when the first paint is already ready or the visitor has seen it this session. **Better:** the plan's Priority 2 item 9, a non-blocking draw-in of the hero lockup.

### U5. Clicking the artwork closes the viewer under a zoom-out cursor (Medium, Code)

**Where:** `ImageStage`, `DocumentStage`, `VideoStage` (`cursor-zoom-out`, `useClickToClose`).
